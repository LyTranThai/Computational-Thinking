## Communication

- **Communication channel:** The main communication channel will be our Messenger group. Messages are expected to be seen within 48 hours. When receiving a message, members who have understood the message should react with some kind of emoji, otherwise, one must ask for clarification directly in the group chat. The team leader is the main contact for the instructor.
- **Meetings:** Weekly meetings are expected to be before lab sessions of this subject, depending on the team situation on that particular week.

## Work process & standards

- **Task management:** Tasks and brainstorming sections will be managed on a shared Google Sheet, the team leader will keep track of the sheet and create reminders to make sure the team is always ahead of deadlines.
- **Code review:** Each member should review other members' code as assigned during the working of this project.
- **Decision making:** The team will decide base on majority votings.

## Conflict resolution

- **Direct communication:** Members with differing opinions or disagreements will first discuss the matter directly and openly to reach a mutual understanding.
- **Team mediation:** If a consensus cannot be reached, the issue will be brought to the team for discussion and settled via majority vote.
- **Escalation:** If a conflict remains unresolved or threatens project progress, the team will consult the instructor or TA for final guidance.

## Academic integrity and AI use

- **Global rules:** All brainstorming, decision making, planning will be mainly done by the members, possibly with some help from AI tools.
- **AI log:** Every AI usage is logged into a weekly log and will be included in the weekly report. Rules for logging AI activities are provided in `AGENT.md` in the root of the project folder.

## Change of contract

- **Proposal:** Any member may propose amendments or additions to this contract at any time via group discussion.
- **Approval:** Proposed changes must be approved by a majority vote of all team members.
- **Updates:** Any approved modifications will be updated in this file and committed to the repository to keep all members aligned.

## Signatures

All members have reviewed the team contract and agree to work with the aforementioned rules.
